Historic

Iconic heritage street

Calle Crisologo

01

This is Vigan's most iconic street, lined with ancestral houses from the Spanish colonial era. Take a walk or a kalesa ride, especially when the street lamps create a warm vintage glow.

History & trivia

The houses are famous for bahay na bato architecture, combining stone ground floors and wooden upper floors with capiz-shell windows.

Historic

Central public square

Plaza Salcedo

02

The city's main public square is surrounded by important government and religious buildings. Its well-known evening attraction is the Dancing Fountain Show.

History & trivia

Plaza Salcedo is named for Juan de Salcedo and is associated with the 1763 execution of revolutionary heroine Gabriela Silang. Its large pool later became the setting for the dancing fountain.

Historic

Food & public life

Plaza Burgos

03

Beside Vigan Cathedral and Plaza Salcedo, this square is a favorite place to relax and try local favorites such as Vigan empanada and okoy.

History & trivia

The plaza is named in honor of native priest and martyr Father Jose Burgos, one of the three GomBurZa priests.

Culture

Religious landmark

Vigan Cathedral

04

A majestic Roman Catholic church dominating the central plaza. Its massive structure follows the Earthquake Baroque style commonly associated with seismic adaptation.

History & trivia

The current stone church was built in the 18th century. Its octagonal bell tower stands apart from the main church building.

Culture

Living craft tradition

Pagburnayan

05

A traditional pottery workshop where visitors can see the long-established method of making burnay earthen jars and try shaping clay on the potter's wheel.

History & trivia

The craft dates back to the pre-colonial era and was influenced by Chinese traders. Burnay jars are traditionally used for products such as basi and bagoong.

History & collections

Ilocos Regional Museum

06

The museum complex presents historical artifacts, Basi Revolt paintings, and ethnographic materials connected with the Ilocos region.

History & trivia

The site includes heritage structures that offer insight into Vigan's past, including stories associated with Father Jose Burgos and former President Elpidio Quirino.

Unique

Wildlife destination

Baluarte Zoo

07

A mini-zoo and wildlife attraction known for a variety of animals, including ostriches, tigers, and exotic birds, with visitor-oriented animal experiences.

History & trivia

The attraction is associated with former Ilocos Sur Governor Luis “Chavit” Singson and is often described as a safari-style destination.
